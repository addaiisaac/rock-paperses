# Reclaiming Priority

A quiet, single-page website for Reclaiming Priority — a movement helping young Christian professionals and students (ages 15–30) trade hurry for an intentional walk with God.

## Open the site

From this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4173
```

Then visit `http://localhost:4173`.

## What is here

- **The wake-up call** — the opening invitation and a sacred pause
- **Conviction** — the shift from busyness to intentionality
- **Digital sanctuary** — a spiritual journey evaluation, a customizable daily plan, and teaching on digital boundaries
- **Community** — a prayer and gratitude wall
- **Partnership** — pray (notes), give (pledge), and serve (writers, designers, technologists)

Forms, the evaluation, the daily plan, and wall notes are kept in this browser (`localStorage`). Nothing is sent to a server, and giving does not charge a card.

Typography is Fraunces and Outfit, both under the SIL Open Font License. License texts are in `fonts/`.
